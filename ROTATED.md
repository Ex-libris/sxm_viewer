The current crop/rotate tool is underdeveloped and underutilized. While the crop template is helpful for previewing adjustments, the native tool found under Image > Crop/Rotate is currently unstable.

Specifically, the tool exhibits strange persistence; changes often remain visible in preview and thumbnail images even after undoing or resetting them. Furthermore, the application of crop and rotation adjustments feels undefined, making it difficult to gauge how they affect the final output.

There are also display issues: when an image is rotated, it is rendered within a frame that is largely occupied by empty space, leaving the rotated image appearing small in the center. Finally, rather than applying edits directly to the original file, the tool should ideally create an adjacent virtual copy to ensure a non-destructive workflow.

However, I believe we could benefit from an overall re-assesment of how it is defined, designed and implemented.
